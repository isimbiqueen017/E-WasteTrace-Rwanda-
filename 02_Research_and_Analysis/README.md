# Research and Analysis

## Introduction

This section focuses on researching electronic waste management in Rwanda and understanding the challenges associated with electronic waste disposal.

## Research Areas

- Electronic waste management in Rwanda.
- Environmental effects of electronic waste.
- Electronic waste collection methods.
- Recycling and waste treatment.
- Technology solutions for tracking electronic waste.

## Research Objectives

- To understand the current e-waste situation in Rwanda.
- To identify challenges in electronic waste management.
- To explore solutions for improving waste collection.
- To identify opportunities for safe recycling.

## Expected Outcomes

The research will help identify problems and opportunities that will guide the development of the E-WasteTrace Rwanda system.
