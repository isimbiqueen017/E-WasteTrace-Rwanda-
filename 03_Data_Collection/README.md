# Data Collection

## Introduction

This section focuses on collecting information and data related to electronic waste management in Rwanda.

## Types of Data

- Types of electronic waste.
- Locations where electronic waste is produced.
- Quantity of electronic waste collected.
- Electronic waste collection centers.
- Recycling facilities.

## Data Collection Methods

- Questionnaires.
- Interviews.
- Observation.
- Research from reliable online sources.

## Objectives

- To identify common electronic waste in Rwanda.
- To understand where electronic waste comes from.
- To collect information about waste collection and recycling.
- To support the development of the E-WasteTrace Rwanda system.

## Expected Outcomes

The collected data will help us understand electronic waste management challenges and develop an effective tracking system.
