# Business Process

## 1. Introduction

This section explains how the E-WasteTrace Rwanda system will operate. It describes the activities involved in reporting, collecting, tracking, and recycling electronic waste.

## 2. Main Actors

- **Users:** Report unwanted electronic waste.
- **Administrator:** Manages reports and monitors collection activities.
- **Collection Team:** Collects electronic waste from reported locations.
- **Recycling Centers:** Receive and process collected electronic waste.

## 3. Business Process Steps

1. User registers on the system.
2. User submits an electronic waste report.
3. Administrator receives and reviews the report.
4. Administrator assigns a collection task.
5. Collection team collects the electronic waste.
6. Collected waste is transported to a recycling center.
7. Recycling center processes the electronic waste.
8. The system updates the waste collection and recycling status.

## 4. Business Process Objectives

- Improve electronic waste collection.
- Make waste reporting easier.
- Track electronic waste movement.
- Improve communication between users and collectors.
- Promote responsible recycling.

## 5. Expected Results

The process aims to improve electronic waste management, increase recycling activities, and reduce environmental pollution in Rwanda.
